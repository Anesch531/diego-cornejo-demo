Who you're training with

A player first,
then a coach

Born in 1986, Chilean and German, speaking Spanish and English. Diego turned a junior career that took him to the Orange Bowl and Eddie Herr into 25 years of coaching — from Viña del Mar to Miami, Kuwait, and now Dubai.

“Tennis defines me as a person in every habit of my life. I have very high energy and I am extremely detail-oriented. I enjoy teaching, being on court, and promoting tennis for people of all ages, backgrounds, and levels.” Diego Cornejo Ernst

The road

Chile to Dubai,
the long way

Junior circuits, national titles, ATP Tour bags, academies in Miami and Kuwait — every stop is why your session works.

  • 1994–95
    International champion of taekwondo, WTF Bariloche, Argentina — before tennis took over.
  • 1997–98
    Champion of the Year, Circuito Campeones para Chile, and 1st place in the national ranking, Valparaíso.
  • 2000
    2nd place, COSAT South America Championship, Venezuela. 5th in the team category, World Cup Championship, Czech Republic.
  • 2005
    #1 in the national ranking, Chile.
  • 2008–09
    Coached Andrea Koch to a #224 WTA ranking.
  • 2012–14
    CEO of Sport Center IST and head coach of high performance at AIT Academy, Viña del Mar, Chile.
  • 2015–16
    Pro tennis and fitness coach at Nuñez Tennis Training, Miami. Coached Bastian Malla to #362 ATP.
  • 2017–18
    Head coach and high-performance fitness coach at Eyzaguirre Tennis Academy, Miami, Florida.
  • 2018–19
    Captain of Chile at the ITF World Tennis Championships, Miami Beach. Coached Juan Carlos Sáez to #230 ATP.
  • 2021
    Head coach at the Rafa Nadal Academy, Kuwait.
  • 2023 NOW
    Founder and director, Cornejo Tennis Academy, Dubai.
